Atlas Abrasives & Coatings is a Utah-local sandblasting company built around one promise: do the prep work right, so your finish lasts.

Led by **Kade Atkinson (Owner/Operator)**, we bring hands-on craftsmanship and accountability to every project.

We handle jobs for homeowners, contractors, and industrial clients who need clean substrate conditions before coatings, sealants, or restoration work begins.
